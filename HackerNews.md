## 4. Best Practices for Hacker News Dominance

1.  **Be Honest & Authentic**: HN readers are highly technical. If they ask how your Twitter scraper works or what AI model you use, be transparent.
2.  **Monitor the Comments**: Keep your Hacker News submissions tab open for the first 3 hours. Reply to every single comment and question politely and constructively.
3.  **Do Not Ask for Votes**: Hacker News has strict vote-manipulation detection. Do not ask friends to log in and upvote your post from the same network; let them find it organically under the "New" or "Show" tab.
